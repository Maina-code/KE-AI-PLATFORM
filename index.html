The Systemic Challenge

Public procurement is robust on paper but vulnerable in practice. Financial leakage, manipulated evaluations, and inflated tenders undermine national development.

⚠️

Tender Manipulation

Rigged specifications and short notice periods exclude competitive bidders and favor pre-selected cronies.

📈

Inflated Contract Values

Goods and services priced significantly above market rates, draining public resources for private gain.

🤝

Conflict of Interest

Officials awarding contracts to companies owned by relatives or associates without disclosure.

Where Corruption Occurs

Vulnerabilities exist at every stage of the procurement lifecycle.

1

Announcement

Hidden advertisements or tailored specs.

2

Evaluation

Biased scoring and disqualification.

3

Award

Ignoring lower bids for "technical reasons".

4

Payment

Paying for unfinished or ghost work.

Oversight & Escalation

How institutions detect and act on irregularities.

🔍 Audit Stage

Auditors review documentation against regulatory frameworks.

  • Flags procedural non-compliance
  • Identifies math errors in bid comparison
  • Verifies value-for-money metrics

⚖️ Investigation Stage

Escalation to anti-corruption bureaus for prosecution.

  • Forensic analysis of bank flows
  • Interviewing whistleblowers
  • Prepares dockets for prosecution
